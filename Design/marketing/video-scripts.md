# Short-form video scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Drafts for owner review. Do not film or post anything until the owner approves each script and has 2 weeks of real sleep-log data to show. Every "app screen" beat means a real screen recording of the actual app, never a mockup presented as real.

Ground rules baked into every script:
- The story is personal: "this happened to me", never "this will happen to you".
- Allowed claim only: helps build a consistent bedtime. No cure, no fix-your-insomnia, no health outcomes.
- No urgency tricks, no guilt hooks, no "you're ruining your life" framing. The viewer is a teen at 3 a.m.; talk to them like a friend, not a parent.
- CTA is always soft: the app is free, no account, link in bio. Nothing expires.
- Low light, quiet voice or captions-only where noted. The videos should feel like the app: dark, calm, honest.

---

## 1. The log (hero video)

**Length:** ~30s. **Format:** screen recording + voiceover.

**Hook (0-2s):** "My bedtime was 5:30 a.m. Not one night. Every night."

**Beats:**
1. (0-3s) Black screen, white text of the hook. Phone clock showing 5:34 AM.
2. (3-10s) Screen recording: scrolling the app's real sleep log, day by day. VO: "So I built an app. All it does is move my bedtime 20 minutes earlier each night and get me off my phone before it."
3. (10-20s) Slow scroll through the log entries: 5:30... 5:10... 4:50... VO: "Here's the actual log. Some nights I missed. The app doesn't care. It just sets tonight's target."
4. (20-27s) Land on a recent entry around 12:40 a.m. Hold. VO: "Took weeks, not days. That's the point."
5. (27-30s) App icon + URL.

**On-screen text:** "every bedtime, logged in the app" over the scroll; "no streaks. a miss just resets tonight's target."

**CTA:** "It's free, no account, no ads. Link in bio if your nights look like mine did."

## 2. POV: 1 a.m. and you're not scrolling

**Length:** ~15s. **Format:** vibe video, captions only, no VO.

**Hook (0-2s):** On-screen: "POV: it's 1 a.m. and you're not scrolling."

**Beats:**
1. (0-4s) Dark room. Phone face-down on a desk, screen glow fading out.
2. (4-9s) Cut to the app's wind-down screen (real, dim) on the phone before it goes face-down: one line, one task.
3. (9-13s) Person actually in bed. Clock: 1:02 AM.
4. (13-15s) Text: "free web app. no account." + URL.

**On-screen text:** "it took me 6 weeks to get here" (small, bottom corner, honest).

**CTA:** none spoken; link in bio.

## 3. Why willpower didn't work

**Length:** ~35s. **Format:** face-to-camera, dim lamp light.

**Hook (0-3s):** "Every night I said 'tonight I'll sleep early.' Every night I went to bed at 5."

**Beats:**
1. (0-6s) Hook to camera, deadpan.
2. (6-15s) "Here's the thing: the phone keeping me up is the same phone I'd need willpower against. That fight was lost every time."
3. (15-26s) Cut to app screen: tonight's single bedtime target. "So instead of willpower, I gave myself one number. One bedtime, tonight. Twenty minutes earlier than yesterday. That's it."
4. (26-33s) "Small enough that I actually did it. The app I built just keeps track and runs a wind-down so I'm not on the feed."
5. (33-35s) URL on screen.

**On-screen text:** "one bedtime. tonight." at beat 3.

**CTA:** "Free, in bio. Built it for myself first."

## 4. The wind-down, shown honestly

**Length:** ~40s. **Format:** screen recording walkthrough, quiet VO.

**Hook (0-3s):** "This is the 30 minutes that gets me off my phone every night. I'll show you all of it."

**Beats:**
1. (0-5s) App opens. Dark screen, one line: tonight's bedtime.
2. (5-15s) Step through the real wind-down: brain dump (type the thoughts out), VO: "First, everything in my head goes here so it stops looping."
3. (15-24s) Breathing step (4-7-8 timer on screen). VO: "Then breathing. It's literally a timer. Nothing to buy, nothing locked."
4. (24-32s) Body-scan step, then "phone away" screen. VO: "Last step is the only hard one: phone goes across the room."
5. (32-40s) Black. Text: "That's the whole app. Free. No account." + URL.

**On-screen text:** step names as they appear.

**CTA:** "Link in bio if you want to try tonight's version of it."

## 5. The $70 question

**Length:** ~30s. **Format:** face-to-camera + screenshots of store pricing pages (real, current).

**Hook (0-3s):** "Sleep apps wanted $70 a year to help me sleep. I was 16. I had $0."

**Beats:**
1. (0-6s) Hook. Hold up phone showing a real subscription price screen (no app ridiculed by name in VO; the screen speaks for itself).
2. (6-14s) "And the reviews on half of them are people fighting to cancel a free trial that charged them."
3. (14-24s) Cut to the app. "So mine is a web app. No account. No ads. No trial, because there's nothing to pay for. Your data stays on your phone."
4. (24-30s) "If it helps, it helps. If not, you close the tab and you've lost nothing." URL.

**On-screen text:** "free. no account. no ads." at beat 3.

**CTA:** link in bio.

**Owner check before filming:** verify the pricing screenshots are current and accurate on the day of filming; no edited or exaggerated screenshots.

## 6. Day 1 vs day 40

**Length:** ~20s. **Format:** split screen / cut between two real log entries.

**Hook (0-2s):** On-screen: "day 1 of moving my bedtime back" over a log entry reading 5:20 AM.

**Beats:**
1. (0-6s) Day 1 log entry. VO or caption: "Day 1: bedtime 5:20 a.m. Target: 5:00."
2. (6-12s) Quick cuts of intermediate entries, dates visible. Caption: "20 to 30 minutes at a time. Some misses in there too."
3. (12-17s) Day ~40 entry: 12:50 a.m. Caption: "Day 40: 12:50."
4. (17-20s) "Built the app myself. It's free." + URL.

**On-screen text:** real dates on every entry. Nothing trimmed to hide misses; a visible missed night makes it more believable, not less.

**CTA:** link in bio.

## 9. Parent-facing cut (same story, different audience)

**Length:** ~35s. **Format:** face-to-camera, daylight, plain.

**Hook (0-3s):** "If your kid is going to sleep at 5 a.m., I was that kid."

**Beats:**
1. (0-8s) Hook. "Lectures didn't move my bedtime. The phone always won."
2. (8-18s) "What worked for me was small steps: one bedtime target each night, a bit earlier than the last, and a wind-down that gets the phone out of my hands."
3. (18-28s) App screens. "I built it into a free web app. No account, no ads, and the data never leaves the phone, which matters when the user is a teenager."
4. (28-35s) "It's not medical anything. It's a routine tool. If sleep problems go on for weeks, that's a doctor conversation, and the app itself says so." URL.

**On-screen text:** "free · no account · data stays on the phone".

**CTA:** "Link below if you want to look at it before showing them."

**Note:** beat 4's doctor line is required; it mirrors the in-app safety prompt and keeps this honest.

## 10. Building it (dev-story angle)

**Length:** ~40s. **Format:** screen recording of code/editor + app, VO.

**Hook (0-3s):** "I couldn't fall asleep before 5 a.m., so I learned to code and built my way out."

**Beats:**
1. (0-8s) Editor with the project open, scrolling. VO: "No app did the one thing I needed: move my bedtime back slowly."
2. (8-18s) VO: "The rule I built in: 15 to 30 minutes earlier at a time. Jumping from 5:30 to midnight in one night fails. I tried."
3. (18-28s) App demo: target, wind-down, morning log. VO: "Dark screens only. The app itself can't be the thing keeping you up."
4. (28-36s) VO: "It's a free web app. Install it from the browser, no store, no account. I use it every night."
5. (36-40s) URL.

**On-screen text:** "built by one person, for one person, shared anyway".

**CTA:** "Link in bio. Tell me what breaks."

## Production notes

- Film 1, 2, and 6 first; they need only the phone and the real log.
- Reuse one honest log recording across videos rather than re-staging it.
- Captions on everything; most viewers watch muted at night.
- Comment replies are part of the content: answer "does it work?" with "it helped me build a consistent bedtime; here's my log" and never with promises.
- If a video takes off, do not add urgency follow-ups ("everyone's downloading it!"). The calm tone is the brand.
