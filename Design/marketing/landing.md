# Landing section copy (one screen, one button)

Copy only; no HTML here per the brief. For later use on the GitHub Pages app page. Dark background, matching the app's night UI. One screen, no scroll required, nothing animated.

## Primary version

**Headline:**
Going to bed at 5 a.m. again?

**Subline:**
One bedtime target a night, a 30-minute wind-down that gets you off your phone, and a plan that moves you earlier a little at a time. Free. No account. No ads. Your data stays on your phone.

**Button:**
Start tonight's wind-down

## Alternate version (story-led)

**Headline:**
I was going to bed at 6 a.m. I built the app that helped me stop.

**Subline:**
It moves your bedtime 15 to 30 minutes earlier each night and runs a wind-down so the phone isn't the last thing you see. No streaks: miss a night and tonight's target just resets. Free, no account, no ads.

**Button:**
Try it tonight

## Fine print (small text under the button, required)

Not medical advice. If sleep problems last for weeks or come with low mood, talk to a doctor.

## Notes for whoever builds it later

- One button only. It opens the app (same page or the app's start screen), not a store, not a signup.
- "Start tonight's wind-down" beats "Get started" because it names the first real action and works at the hour the visitor is most likely reading this.
- No testimonials, counters, or "join X users" social proof; with an under-18 audience and a no-tracking promise we shouldn't be counting users anyway.
- No urgency language anywhere. The app is free and will still be free tomorrow; the copy should read that way.
- Keep the fine print visible without scrolling. It mirrors the in-app safety text from DesignNotes/Safety and Compliance.md.
