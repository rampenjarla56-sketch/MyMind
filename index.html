      Phone setup

      MyMind can't close other apps. Your phone can. Set this up once and the feeds go quiet on their own every night.

      Which phone?

      iPhone

      1. Downtime. Settings → Screen Time → Downtime. Turn on Scheduled, every day, from --:-- to --:--. That's your wind-down start to your wake time.
      2. Always Allowed. Settings → Screen Time → Always Allowed. Keep only Phone, Messages and Clock. Everything else goes dark during Downtime.
      3. App Limits. Settings → Screen Time → App Limits → Add Limit. Pick the two or three feeds that keep you up the most and give them a daily limit you'd actually accept.
      4. Sleep Focus. Settings → Focus → Sleep. Turn on the schedule with the same times, so notifications stay silent too.
      5. Charger across the room. Not a setting, but the one that works. The phone sleeps where you can't reach it from bed.

      Android

      1. Bedtime mode. Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Bedtime mode. Use a schedule, every day, from --:-- to --:--. That's your wind-down start to your wake time.
      2. Grayscale. In Bedtime mode, turn on Grayscale. A grey feed is a boring feed; most people stop scrolling without trying.
      3. Focus mode. Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Focus mode. Add the apps that keep you up and set the same schedule.
      4. App timers. Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Dashboard. Tap the hourglass next to each feed and give it a daily limit you'd actually accept.
      5. Charger across the room. Not a setting, but the one that works. The phone sleeps where you can't reach it from bed.

      Menu names vary a little by brand (Samsung calls it Modes and Routines → Sleep). Search Settings for "bedtime" if you don't see it.

      These are your phone's own tools. MyMind never sees, blocks or controls other apps, and can't tell whether you set this up. The times above follow your plan, so if your bedtime moves, nudge the schedule earlier now and then.

      Erase everything?

      Your plan, every logged night and your settings will be removed from this phone. There is no undo. Export a copy first if you want one.
