## Repro steps

### 1. Major — in-night check-in writes to the night in progress
State: plan `cur 05:30 · goal 00:30 · wake 09:00 · step 30 · start 2026-10-10`, log `[2026-10-11 07:00 (late), 2026-10-12 05:40 (on time)]`. Clock fixed at **2026-10-13 02:00** (the teen is up; tonight's bedtime, night Oct 12, is 05:00).
1. Home shows "Tonight's bedtime 05:00 · Wind-down starts in 2 h 30 min" **and**, two cards down, "Morning check-in — Last night's plan was 05:00. When did you actually fall asleep?" — the same plan as "tonight", because `renderHome` derives "last night" from `addDays(localDate(), −1)` while `currentNight` says the current night *is* that date.
2. Enter 06:30, tap Save. Stored: `{date:'2026-10-13', slept:'06:30', onTime:false}` → that is night Oct 12, i.e. the night that has not happened yet. Home now says "Logged for last night: fell asleep 06:30 · later than planned."
3. Clock to 2026-10-13 22:00: tonight's bedtime is **05:00 again** (HOLD) instead of 04:30, and "about 9 more nights" did not move.
A 5 a.m.-bedtime teen is awake between midnight and bedtime every night; that is the window where a user who forgot the morning log would naturally "catch up". If they log again later that morning the same-date entry is replaced (self-heals), but if they don't, the wrong star, the wrong on-time verdict and the HOLD stand. Fix options: hide/relabel the check-in while `currentNight(...).inNight` is true ("You're still in tonight — log it after you wake"), or make "last night" in the prompt and in `saveLog` use the night *before* `currentNight().nightDate`. Owner: Agent 02, with Agent 03 for the date rule.

### 2. Major — wake time earlier than the bedtime is accepted and praised
1. Setup: cur 05:30, goal 02:00, **wake 01:00**, step 15. No inline error, guardrail hidden (`sleepOpportunity(nightMin(02:00), 01:00)` wraps to 23 h → `ok`).
2. "Start my plan" → home: "Wind-down at 05:00 · wake at 01:00", **"Tonight leaves about 19.5 h for sleep."**
3. By the time model, `currentNight` flips the night date at the wake time, so from 01:00 to 05:30 home already shows *tomorrow's* bedtime; and `checkAlarm` fires at 01:00–01:30 whenever the dark screen is open (60 s after entry), i.e. during the lights-out the user has just started. (Verified the arithmetic and the home copy; the alarm path is the same `checkAlarm` verified in finding 6.)
`validatePlan` only checks goal ≤ current. It should also reject (or at least warn on) `wake` falling inside `(goal … current]` in night minutes, and `opportunityCheck` should never report > 16 h as "room for it". Owner: Agent 03 (`domain.js`), copy in Agent 02.

### 3. Minor — afternoon slept times count as "on time"
Clock 2026-10-14 10:00, last night's plan 05:00. Save **12:00** → "fell asleep 12:00 · on time"; save **15:30** → "on time". `nightMin(720..1199)` is below any planned bedtime, so `isOnTime` passes. Round-1 Note 15 flagged the chart clamp; the module build now also awards the star. Suggest refusing or asking to confirm a slept time between 12:00 and ~18:00.

### 4. Minor — alarm is loud instantly
`ringAlarm` ramps `g.gain` 0.0001 → 0.3 over 120 s, then connects an LFO with `lg.gain = 0.5` straight into `g.gain`. Rendered offline with the same graph: peak |sample| in the first second = **0.500**, at 5–6 s 0.500, at 10–12 s 0.500. The gain also swings negative (phase-inverted, same loudness). Net: a 523 Hz tone at amplitude 0.5 pulsing at 0.5 Hz from t = 0 — louder than the intended ceiling and not gentle. Fix: modulate with `lg.gain` ≈ 0.3 × current level (or scale the LFO by a second gain that follows the ramp), and offset so the gain never crosses 0.

### 5. Minor — focus lost after dismissing the alarm
Clock 2026-10-14 09:05, alarm on, enter the dark screen; after 61 s the wake form appears (grace works). Type ` uP ` → "Tick the curtains box first" (correct); tick the box → home. `document.activeElement` is still **`curtainsDone`** (inside the hidden `#dark`), not `#slept`: `tryDismissAlarm` calls `slept.focus()` synchronously while `main` is still `inert` (the MutationObserver that clears it runs after). Fix: clear `inert` synchronously in `leaveDark()`/`toHome()` before focusing, or focus in a `queueMicrotask`.

### 6. Minor — alarm under an open sheet
Clock 2026-10-15 09:00:30, enter dark, open "I can't sleep", advance 61 s, trigger `visibilitychange`. Result: `#cant` still open, `#alarmBox` visible underneath, focus on `curtainsDone` (outside the sheet the user sees). Fix: in `checkAlarm` bail while `cant`/`panic` are open (or close them first).

### 7. Minor — early wind-down parks on a lit screen for hours
Seed `windDown: {startedAt: now − 31 min, stepIndex: 4}` at 21:35 with bedtime 03:30 → resumes on "Settle" reading **"lights out in 6 h"**; the same happens by tapping "Start wind-down" at 21:30. The settle sheet is full-brightness navy. Suggest a line + button on the settle step when `toDark > 60 min` ("Bedtime is hours away — put the phone away now and come back at 03:00"), or go dark at `anchor + 30 min` regardless and let the clock screen carry the wait.

### 8. Minor — HOLD without a word
Same state as finding 1, clock 2026-10-11 22:00: tonight shows 05:30 (correct HOLD after the 07:00 night), "about 10 more nights"; the only hint is "later than planned. Tonight still counts." Nothing says the bedtime is repeating on purpose. One sentence in `planinfo` ("Last night ran late, so tonight repeats 05:30 — then we keep going") would stop it reading as a bug. (HOLD itself verified: the next on-time night resumed to 05:00.)
