# Launch and marketing
**Audience:** teens/students with phone-driven late nights; parents who worry about it.
**Story:** "I was going to bed at 6 a.m. I built the app that fixed it." Real, personal, filmable.
**Plan**
1. Dogfood 2 weeks; capture real before/after bedtimes (with the sleep log).
2. Short-form video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts): day-by-day bedtime shift, "POV: it's 1 a.m. and you're not scrolling".
3. Post in student communities (Reddit sleep/teenagers, Discord servers) – share the story, not an ad.
4. Free, no account, no ads. Optional parent-share link later.
5. Landing page on GitHub Pages: one screen, one button.
**Rules:** no medical claims, no "cure"; claims = "helps build a consistent bedtime". Under-13 / under-18 privacy rules apply – see Safety.
