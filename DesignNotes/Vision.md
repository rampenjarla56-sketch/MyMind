# Vision
**Problem:** Late-night scrolling pushes bedtime later every night until it is 5–6 a.m. Willpower alone fails because the phone is the thing keeping you up.
**Promise:** Tonight you get one clear bedtime, a 30-minute wind-down that takes you off the feed, and a plan that moves you earlier a little each night until you reach 12–1 a.m.
**First user:** the builder. If it doesn't help him sleep, it's not done.
**Principles**
1. One thing at a time. No dashboards at night.
2. Gradual, not heroic. Jumping from 5:30 to 12:30 in a day doesn't work.
3. Dark, dim, quiet UI. Nothing in the app can itself keep you up.
4. Honest. No streak guilt; a missed night just resets tonight's target.
