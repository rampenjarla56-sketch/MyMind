# Sleep approach (what the app actually does)
Based on common behavioural-sleep guidance (CBT-I basics, delayed sleep phase advice). Not medical advice.
- **Fixed wake time** (even weekends, within ~1h). The anchor that makes earlier sleep possible.
- **Shift bedtime earlier 30 min/night** (15 if it's too hard). From 5:30 → 12:30 takes ~14 nights.
- **Morning light** within 30 min of waking (open curtains / go outside). Biggest lever for shifting the clock.
- **Screens off at wind-down start**; phone charges across the room, not in bed.
- **Brain dump** – write tomorrow's worries/to-dos so the head empties.
- **4-7-8 / slow breathing**, then **body relaxation**.
- **Bed is for sleep.** If not asleep after ~20 min, get up, do something dull in dim light, return when sleepy.
- Caffeine/energy drinks: none after ~2 p.m.
- Teens need ~8–10 h. If the goal is 12:30 sleep, wake ≈ 8:30–9:00, or earlier if school needs it.
- **Safety:** if trouble persists for weeks, or there's low mood/anxiety, talk to a parent/doctor. The app says this.
