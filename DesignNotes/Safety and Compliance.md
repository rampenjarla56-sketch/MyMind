# Safety and compliance
- Not a medical device; show "not medical advice" in Settings/About.
- Prompt to see a doctor if insomnia lasts weeks or low mood/anxiety.
- Data stays on-device (localStorage). No accounts, no tracking, no ads → simplest for teen privacy (COPPA/GDPR-K friendly).
- No streak shame, no engagement-bait. The app must not be another scroll hook.
